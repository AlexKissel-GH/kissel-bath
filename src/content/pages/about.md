Kissel's Bath and Kitchen started as a small family operation with one simple goal: treat every home like our own. Founded by Alex and Daria Kissel, we began with bathroom renovations in the Denver metro area and quickly grew by word-of-mouth — one satisfied homeowner at a time.

Today, we've completed hundreds of bathroom and kitchen remodels across Denver, Aurora, Broomfield, Parker, and Highlands Ranch. What hasn't changed is our commitment to transparency, quality, and respect for your home.

We don't low-bid to win jobs and then surprise you with hidden charges. We don't drag projects out for weeks. And we never cut corners on materials — we use only trusted brands like Kohler, Delta, Moen, Sentrel, and Samuel Mueller.
