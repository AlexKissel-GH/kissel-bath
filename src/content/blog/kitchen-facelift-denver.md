Not every dated kitchen needs a $30,000 gut job. If your layout works, your cabinets are sound, and your appliances still have life in them, a kitchen facelift can deliver the fresh, updated look you're after in 3–5 days — starting at $7,900. It's the fastest, least disruptive way to transform how your kitchen looks and feels, and it's one of the most popular projects we do for homeowners across Denver, Aurora, and the metro. Here's what's involved and how to tell if it's right for you.

## What a Kitchen Facelift Actually Includes

A facelift targets everything you see and touch, while leaving the bones of the kitchen alone. A typical scope includes:

- **New countertops** — the visual anchor of the whole room
- **New backsplash** — tile or other finishes that set the style
- **New sink and faucet** — modern, hard-water-resistant finishes
- **New cabinet hardware** — pulls, knobs, and soft-close hinges
- **Paint and finish touch-ups** — walls, trim, and cabinet touch-ups as scoped

No demolition. No moving plumbing or electrical. No permits. The exact scope for your kitchen is spelled out line by line in your fixed, itemized quote after a free in-home estimate — you'll know precisely what's included before we touch a thing. If your cabinets need more than hardware and touch-ups, [cabinet refacing](/blog/cabinet-refacing-vs-replacement-denver) starting at $10,500 replaces doors and drawer fronts entirely, and it pairs naturally with a facelift scope.

## What a Kitchen Facelift Costs in Denver

Our kitchen facelift starts at $7,900 with a 3–5 day timeline. What moves the number within that range:

- **Kitchen size** — more linear feet of countertop and backsplash means more material

Compared to the alternatives, the value is stark. A full kitchen remodel is a custom-quoted project running 7–14 days and well into five figures when layout or mechanical work is involved. ## Facelift vs. Full Remodel: Which Do You Need?

| Factor | Kitchen facelift | Full remodel |
|---|---|---|
| Starting cost | from $7,900 | Custom quote |
| Timeline | 3–5 days | 7–14 days |
| Layout changes | No | Yes |
| Permits | None | Required for plumbing/electrical/structural |
| Disruption | Minimal | Kitchen offline |
| Best for | Sound bones, dated surfaces | Broken layout, failing cabinets |

The honest test: stand in your kitchen and ask whether the *room* bothers you or the *finishes* do. If it's the finishes — the counters, the colors, the hardware — a facelift solves it. If it's the room itself, you need a bigger conversation.

## Why Facelifts Work So Well in Denver's Ranch Homes

The Denver metro is full of 1950s–70s ranch homes — Aurora, Arvada, Lakewood, and Centennial are packed with them — plus older bungalows closer to downtown. These kitchens share a pattern: closed-off galley layouts, worn laminate counters, dated backsplashes, and cabinet boxes that are often better built than what you'd buy new today.

That combination is practically made for the facelift approach. The surfaces are the problem, not the structure. New counters, a fresh backsplash, modern hardware, and updated lighting transform these spaces completely — and because the footprint stays put, the project stays fast, affordable, and permit-free. We've completed 200+ projects across the metro, and ranch-home kitchens are some of the most dramatic before-and-afters we deliver.

One local note: hard water along the Front Range destroys cheap faucet finishes within a couple of years. We steer facelift clients toward finishes proven to resist mineral spotting, so the kitchen still looks new long after we're gone.

## The 3–5 Day Process, Step by Step

Here's what a facelift week actually looks like:

1. **Day 1 — Protection and prep.** Floors and adjacent rooms protected, old counters and backsplash removed, surfaces prepped.
2. **Day 2 — Countertop installation.** New counters templated (or pre-templated) and set, sink and faucet fitted.
3. **Days 3–4 — Backsplash, hardware, and finishes.** Backsplash installed, hardware mounted, paint and trim touch-ups completed.
4. **Day 5 — Detailing and walkthrough.** Final adjustments, full cleanup, and a walkthrough with you before we call it done.

Throughout, we're a licensed and insured general contractor and an EPA Lead-Safe Certified Firm (NAT-F212803-1) — important in pre-1978 homes, where disturbing old paint without proper containment is a genuine health issue. Our work carries a 1-year labor warranty.
