Most of our bathroom projects are completed in 3–5 days. A budget shower renovation takes 2–3 days, a complete remodel up to 50 sq.ft. takes 5–12 days, and a full kitchen remodel typically takes 7–14 days. We don't drag projects out — we respect your time and your home.
