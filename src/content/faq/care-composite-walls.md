---
question: "How do I care for my new composite shower walls?"
category: "Warranty & Aftercare"
order: 18
---

Sentrel and Samuel Mueller composite walls are extremely low-maintenance. Simply wipe them down with a non-abrasive cleaner and a soft cloth. No scrubbing grout, no sealing, no special treatments. The non-porous surface resists mold, mildew, and stains naturally.
