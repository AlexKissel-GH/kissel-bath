---
question: "What is a curbless (barrier-free) shower?"
category: "Accessibility & Special Needs"
order: 15
---

A curbless shower has zero threshold — the shower floor is level with the bathroom floor. This eliminates the tripping hazard of a traditional tub or shower curb, making it ideal for wheelchair users, seniors, or anyone with mobility concerns. We use specialized waterproofing and sloped bases to ensure perfect drainage.
