---
question: "What are Sentrel composite walls, and why do you use them?"
category: "Products & Materials"
order: 9
---

Sentrel is a multi-layered composite material designed to look like natural stone or marble but without the maintenance. It's non-porous, resists mold and mildew, requires no grout, and comes with a lifetime warranty. It's made in the USA and installs much faster than tile — which means less disruption in your home.
